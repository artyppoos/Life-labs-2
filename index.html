01 — The Idea

Our Streaming Concept

Our group plans to create a product and food review livestream that combines honest opinions with useful information.

Check Before You Choose

Our livestream will review different products and food based on factors such as quality, price, taste, usefulness, and safety. Instead of simply saying whether something is good or bad, we want to provide information that viewers can consider before buying or trying something.


Through our website and livestream, we hope to create content that is both entertaining and helpful while encouraging viewers to make safer and more informed choices.

02 — Our Viewers

Who We're Creating This For

Understanding our viewers helps us decide what information should be included in our reviews and how we should present it.

01

Our Audience

Teens and young viewers who are interested in products, food, and online reviews.

02

What They Need

Clear, honest, relevant, and easy-to-understand information before making a choice.

03

What Makes a Good Stream

A good stream should be informative, interactive, organized, entertaining, honest, and responsible.

03 — Development

From Ideas to Our Final Concept

Our concept changed as we compared different ideas and considered what would be most useful to our audience.

Travel Vlogs

We first considered travel-related content because it could be entertaining and visually interesting.

Day-in-the-Life

We explored a more personal type of content that would show everyday experiences.

Community Helping

We considered content that could focus on helping people and highlighting community experiences.

Cooking Tutorials

We also considered cooking content because food is relatable and naturally suited for livestreaming.

Product & Food Reviews

We eventually chose product and food reviews because they allow us to combine entertainment with information that viewers can actually use when making decisions.

04 — Setup

Platform, Tools & Equipment

We selected tools that can support clear communication, good visuals, and an organized livestream.

Tool / Equipment Purpose
YouTube Main livestreaming platform for presenting our reviews and interacting with viewers.
Google Sites Website platform for organizing our project information, content, and sources.
Smartphone / Camera Used to record the livestream and capture visuals.
Microphone / Headset Helps make the hosts' voices clearer.
Light Source Improves visibility and the overall quality of the video.
Stable Internet Helps keep the livestream smooth and connected.
Canva Used to create thumbnails, graphics, and other visuals.

05 — Content

What Happens During the Stream

Our livestream will have different activities so that the content stays useful and interactive.

01

Product Review

Discuss the product's purpose, quality, price, usefulness, and other important factors.

02

Food Review

Talk about taste, quality, price, overall experience, and other relevant details.

03

Compare & Decide

Compare two similar choices and discuss their strengths and differences.

04

Viewer Questions

Respond to audience questions, comments, and reactions during the livestream.

05

Final Takeaway

Summarize the important points viewers should consider before making their own choice.

06 — Our Plan

SMART Goals

Our goals help keep the project specific, realistic, and connected to the purpose of our livestream.

S

Specific

Create a product and food review livestream focused on useful information.

M

Measurable

Prepare at least one product review and one food review with researched information.

A

Achievable

Use accessible tools such as smartphones, laptops, Canva, and Google Sites.

R

Relevant

Help viewers become more informed and careful before buying or trying something.

T

Time-Bound

Finalize our website content and livestream plan by our next group meeting.
